[openvex/spec at commit 61b5f885d0f481f48683c93345e49ef1a6e9fdff](https://github.com/openvex/spec/blob/61b5f885d0f481f48683c93345e49ef1a6e9fdff/openvex_json_schema.json),


The schema declares OpenVEX 0.2.0 and JSON Schema 2020-12. The upstream project
uses [CC0-1.0](https://github.com/openvex/spec/blob/main/LICENSE).
`assessment-proposal.schema.json` is this companion's strict internal v1 schema.
It is not an OpenVEX extension. Both schemas are bundled into the investigator
and validated with networknt `json-schema-validator` 1.5.9.
